# Third-party art

Pythime uses CC0 packs as a legal base/reference for urban pixel art. The downloaded files are ignored by Git and are installed locally by the Unity editor.

Use:

`Pythime > Install or Update CC0 Art Packs`

## Kenney RPG Urban Pack

- Author: Kenney
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-urban-pack
- Original source: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-urban-pack
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
- Tile size: 16x16
- Use in Pythime: runtime Tilemap details, cars, trees and urban props

## Kenney Roguelike Modern City

- Author: Kenney
- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/roguelike-modern-city
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
- Use in Pythime: city/building reference and future Tilemap replacement of procedural facades

## Kenney Roguelike Indoors

- Author: Kenney
- Source: https://kenney.nl/assets/roguelike-indoors
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
- Use in Pythime: interiors, rooms, furniture and indoor exploration

## Direction

The packs are used because their licenses allow adaptation and commercial use. Pythime does not copy maps, characters, logos or proprietary sprite sheets from The Escapists. The reference is limited to general top-down readability, compact character proportions, fake perspective, limited tones and dense interactive environments.
