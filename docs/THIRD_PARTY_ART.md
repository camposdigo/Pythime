# Third-party art

## Kenney RPG Urban Pack

Pythime can automatically download the **Kenney RPG Urban Pack** for development/reference through `Pythime > Install CC0 Urban Art` in the Unity editor.

- Author: Kenney
- Source: https://opengameart.org/content/rpg-urban-pack
- Original source: https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-urban-pack
- License: Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)
- Tile size: 16x16

The downloaded pack is intentionally ignored by Git. The game-specific artwork and map composition remain original to Pythime; the pack is used as a legal CC0 base/reference rather than copying assets from The Escapists.
